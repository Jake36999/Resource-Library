# Glossary - Chunking

## Definition
Dividing a document into the units that get indexed and returned. Usually treated as preprocessing and usually the decision that most affects retrieval quality: split at a structural boundary and a result is a whole thing, split by character count and it can begin mid-sentence while reading as complete.

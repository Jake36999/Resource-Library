# Glossary - Research Compendium

## Definition
A self-contained directory holding an analysis together with its data, its code, its environment definition and its narrative, so the result can be re-executed rather than only re-read. The environment is the part usually missing, and the part whose absence makes reproduction impossible.

- reproducible compendium
- executable paper

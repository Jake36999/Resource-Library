# Pattern - SaaS Product Design

## Definition
Multi-user design work hosted as a service, where shared component libraries and tokens are the contract between design and front-end implementation.

## Why It Matters
- Gives the vault a reusable architectural concept for graph traversal.
- Helps compare repositories that solve the same problem in different ways.

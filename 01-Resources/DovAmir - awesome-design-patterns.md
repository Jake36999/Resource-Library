# DovAmir - awesome-design-patterns

## Bottom Line
An awesome-list index of design pattern catalogues spanning classical GoF patterns, cloud and microservice patterns, and language-specific collections.

## What It Solves
- Find the right pattern catalogue for a given layer instead of searching per-language.
- Compare how the same structural idea is named across communities.
- Provide a starting map of pattern literature for the catalogue's pattern layer.

## Architecture & Mechanics
- Entries are grouped by pattern family: design, architecture, cloud, microservices, language-specific.
- Each entry is a single annotated link rather than inline content.
- Follows the awesome-list contribution and formatting conventions.
- Last upstream push was October 2024 — links should be spot-checked before relying on them.

## What Is Inside
- **Three files.** `README.md` is the entire resource; `contributing.md` and `_config.yml` are the rest.
- **What the list covers** — design patterns organised by language and by domain, pointing outward to books, articles and repositories.
- **Nothing is vendored**, so the list decays exactly as fast as its targets do, and **there is no licence in any form** — the compilation carries no grant. Catalogued as a discovery aid, not as material to reuse.

## Transferable Capability
**Index the same body of knowledge along two axes at once — by concept and by language — so it can be entered from either direction.** Someone who knows the pattern and wants an implementation, and someone who knows the language and wants to know which patterns are idiomatic in it, are different readers arriving at the same material.

**Alternative to:** a single ordering, which serves whichever reader the author had in mind. **Applies wherever** one body of material has more than one natural entry point — and it is a modest example of the argument in [[Use Contexts And Agnostic Description]] that description must anticipate more than one context of use.

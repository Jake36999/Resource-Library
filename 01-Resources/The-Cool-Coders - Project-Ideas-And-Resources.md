# The-Cool-Coders - Project-Ideas-And-Resources

## Bottom Line
A catalogue of build-it-yourself project briefs graded beginner, intermediate and advanced, each with objectives, user stories, bonus features and learning resources — aimed at developers who want practice and cannot decide what to make.

## What It Solves
- Remove the blank-page problem when learning by building.
- Give a project enough specification to be finishable rather than open-ended.
- Offer a graded progression rather than a flat list.

## Architecture & Mechanics
- Projects are tiered beginner, intermediate and advanced, with a separate blockchain and DApp section.
- Each entry carries objectives, user stories, bonus features and links to relevant resources.
- The user-story framing gives each project a definition of done.
- Entries are contributed and maintained by pull request.

## What Is Inside
- **Three files, one of which matters** — `README.md` is the entire resource.
- **What it contains** — project ideas grouped by difficulty and domain, each with a short brief and links to reference implementations or to the APIs that would supply the data.
- **Nothing is vendored and nothing is executable.** Its use is as a prompt when scoping a learning project, not as material to reuse.

## Transferable Capability
**Pair a problem statement with the material needed to attempt it**, so a proposal is actionable rather than aspirational. Naming the data source or reference implementation alongside the idea removes the step where most attempts stop.

**Alternative to:** a list of ideas, where every entry carries an unstated research cost before anything can begin. **Applies wherever** somebody must choose among things to build and the choice depends on what is already available to build with.

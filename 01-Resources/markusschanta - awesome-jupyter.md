# markusschanta - awesome-jupyter

## Bottom Line
A curated index of roughly 150 Jupyter-ecosystem projects, organised into thirteen categories from runtimes and frontends through visualisation, version control and hosted notebook services.

## What It Solves
- Find the right Jupyter tool without searching each category separately.
- See the ecosystem's shape — where it is dense and where it is thin.
- Discover extensions that solve notebook problems already solved by someone.

## Architecture & Mechanics
- Thirteen top-level categories: runtimes and frontends, collaboration and education, visualisation, tables, rendering and publishing, version control, JupyterLab extensions, testing, domain-specific projects, hosted solutions, official and community resources, and articles.
- The JupyterLab extensions section is the largest, with over forty entries covering formatting, debugging and AI integration.
- Each entry is an annotated link rather than inline content.
- Roughly 150 entries in total, maintained by pull request.

## What Is Inside
- **Nine files; `README.md` is the resource.** A curated list of Jupyter-ecosystem projects: kernels, extensions, deployment, teaching material, and hosted services.
- **Two CI workflows** — `main.yml` (link checking) and `traffic.yml` (recording repository traffic), which is a small, copyable pattern for keeping a link list from rotting.
- **Nothing is vendored.** Every entry is outbound, and the list decays with its targets.
- **Licence is CC-BY-SA-4.0** — copyleft for content, so a derivative list must share alike.

## Transferable Capability
**Automate the decay check that a compilation of references otherwise never gets.** Every entry points outward, so the collection's real failure mode is silent rot; a scheduled check of whether the targets still exist is the smallest thing that keeps it honest.

**Alternative to:** trusting that entries remain valid, which is true on the day of writing and progressively less true afterwards. **Applies to every collection of outbound references**, including any catalogue whose value depends on its pointers still resolving.

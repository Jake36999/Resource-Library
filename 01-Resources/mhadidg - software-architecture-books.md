# mhadidg - software-architecture-books

## Bottom Line
A ranked bibliography of software architecture books, sorted by Goodreads rating and review count, with publication year and rating shown for each entry.

## What It Solves
- Choose architecture reading by evidence of reception rather than marketing.
- See at a glance which canonical texts are dated versus current.
- Give a reading path to engineers moving into architecture roles.

## Architecture & Mechanics
- A single sortable table lists title, author, year, rating and review count.
- Ratings are sourced from Goodreads at the time of the last update.
- Entries link out to the book record rather than embedding summaries.
- Last upstream push was March 2023 — ratings and coverage are a point-in-time snapshot.

## What Is Inside
- **One file.** `README.md` is the entire repository: a bibliography of software architecture books, each with publication year and a short note, ordered so the list can be read as a reading path rather than an index.
- **No licence file** — the licence is stated as CC-BY-4.0 in the readme itself; see `Reading Notes`.
- **Nothing is vendored and nothing is executable.** Its use is choosing what to read next, and it is deliberately small enough to scan in one sitting.

## Transferable Capability
**Order a bibliography as a path rather than as an index**, so the collection answers *what should I read next* and not only *what exists*. Sequencing is an editorial act that a sorted list refuses to perform, and it is the part a reader cannot reconstruct.

**Alternative to:** an alphabetical or chronological listing, which is complete and useless for choosing. **Applies wherever** a body of material has prerequisites and somebody has to start somewhere.

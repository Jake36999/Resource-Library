# armanakbari - Awsome-Efficient-DLMs

## Bottom Line
A bibliography of research papers on making diffusion language models efficient, sorted into technique categories — sampling, KV cache, distillation, flow matching, reasoning — with dates, links and venue badges per entry.

## What It Solves
- Track a fast-moving research area without following every venue.
- Group papers by the technique they use rather than by publication date.
- See which approaches to efficiency are attracting the most work.

## Architecture & Mechanics
- Entries are grouped into thematic sections: sampling, KV cache, distillation, flow matching, reasoning, and unsorted.
- Each entry carries a publication date, a link and a badge indicating venue such as ArXiv, NeurIPS or ICLR.
- The scope is specifically diffusion language models rather than diffusion models generally.
- Maintained by addition as papers appear.

## What Is Inside
- **One file.** `README.md` is the entire repository — no licence, no configuration, nothing else.
- **What it contains** — a reading list on efficient diffusion language models: papers grouped by technique, with links out to arXiv and to authors' code.
- **Nothing is vendored and nothing is executable.** Catalogued as a survey pointer; the papers it names are the material, and it carries no grant of its own.

## Transferable Capability
**Group a research literature by the technique it employs rather than by date or venue**, so the collection answers *what approaches exist to this problem* — the question a newcomer actually has. Chronological or citation orderings answer questions only someone already inside the field would ask.

**Alternative to:** a reading list ordered by recency, which reflects publication rather than structure. **Applies wherever** a body of research must be entered by someone who does not yet know its divisions.

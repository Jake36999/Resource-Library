# moyu6027 - awsome-chatgpt-like

## Bottom Line
A Chinese-language survey of open-source ChatGPT-style fine-tuned models, organised by base model family and extending into the applications, frameworks and datasets built around each.

## What It Solves
- Map which fine-tuned models derive from which base model.
- Find the datasets and frameworks associated with a model family, not just the weights.
- Cover Chinese-language work on open models that English lists often miss.

## Architecture & Mechanics
- Organised hierarchically by major base model, beginning with LLaMA and its derivatives.
- Sections extend from models into applications, frameworks and datasets.
- Implementation approaches such as C++ inference are covered alongside the models.
- Written in Chinese with English translations provided throughout.

## What Is Inside
- **One file.** `README.md` is the entire repository — no licence, no configuration, no assets.
- **What it contains** — a list of open-source alternatives and clones in the ChatGPT space, grouped by kind (model, UI, self-hosted stack).
- **Fast-decaying by construction.** Every entry is an outbound link in a field that moved substantially after the list was compiled, so treat entries as leads to verify rather than as current facts. No licence means the compilation itself carries no grant.

## Transferable Capability
**Enumerate the alternatives to a dominant thing, which is the enumeration nobody produces by default.** Attention concentrates on the leading option, so the fact that substitutes exist at all is the scarce information — even when the individual entries age badly.

**Alternative to:** evaluating the obvious choice on its own merits, where the question *what else does this job* is never asked. **Applies directly to the failure this catalogue exists to counteract** — see [[Use Contexts And Agnostic Description]] on stewardship: a list of alternatives is worth having precisely because the default is to optimise the thing already chosen.
